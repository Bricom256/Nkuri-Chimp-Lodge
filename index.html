About the Lodge

Nkuri Chimp Lodge is a small lodge beautifully set in the village of Nkingo located in Kamwenge/Fort Portal district at the borders of Kibale National Park.

Kibale National Park is one of Uganda’s top primate destinations home to about 1,450 chimpanzees. It also boasts over 375 bird species and many crater lakes making it one of the country’s most remarkable destinations.

The lodge is only a 5-minute drive from Kanyanchu Visitor Centre where chimpanzee tracking begins.

Located in the community village of Kishojo, a common hub for forest elephants, the lodge was born from the unique relationship between tourism, agriculture and the surrounding wildlife.

You may even experience elephants visiting the lodge at night. A variety of trees around the property creates a beautiful forest that attracts many bird species singing harmoniously.

Location

Nkuri Chimp Lodge is just 700 meters off the main road on the Fort Portal–Kamwenge Road. After the park boundary when getting out of the forest, you will see our signpost and branch off to your right.

When coming from Mbarara, just after Bigodi town and about 400 meters before reaching the park, you will see our signpost on your left. A short one-minute drive leads you directly to the lodge.
